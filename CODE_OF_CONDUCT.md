# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for everyone participating in `photo_gallery_gdx_plus`, regardless of background, identity, experience, or level of expertise.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and considerate.
- Discuss ideas and code without personal attacks.
- Accept feedback gracefully and focus on the project’s technical needs.
- Respect privacy, attribution, intellectual property, and licensing obligations.
- Help make participation safe and accessible for other contributors.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct, sustained disruption, or other abusive behavior is not acceptable in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to `contact@gurwinderdevx.com`. Reports will be reviewed fairly and kept confidential as far as practical. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, reject participation, issue warnings, or temporarily or permanently restrict access when behavior violates this policy.

This policy applies in repository discussions, issues, Pull Requests, reviews, and other spaces where someone is representing the project.
